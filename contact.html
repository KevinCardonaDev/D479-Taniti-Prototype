Emergency Services

Taniti has one hospital and several clinics to assist residents and visitors with medical needs.

Local Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English, especially in tourist areas.

Currency

The U.S. dollar is the primary currency used on the island. Major credit cards are accepted in many locations.

Frequently Asked Questions

• Drinking age: 18 years old
• Power outlets: 120 volts (same as the United States)
• Many businesses close during national holidays.
