Places to Stay

Find lodging options for every type of traveler, from simple stays to resort comfort.

Taniti offers a wide variety of lodging, including an inexpensive hostel, family-owned hotels, bed and breakfasts, private stays, and one large four-star resort.

Four-Star Resort

Visitors looking for a premium stay can choose Taniti's large four-star resort with comfortable accommodations.

Family-Owned Hotels

Many small, family-owned hotels provide visitors with a welcoming and local island experience.

Bed and Breakfasts

A growing number of bed and breakfasts offer cozy lodging options for tourists who want a more personal stay.

Hostel

Budget-conscious travelers can choose an inexpensive hostel while exploring the island.

Regulated Lodging

All lodging options are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.
